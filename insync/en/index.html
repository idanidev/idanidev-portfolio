The calendar for both of you

One person's work shifts, the other's plans, the shopping, the errands and the summer trip. In one place, and the same on both phones.

iPhone and Android — because if only one of you can install it, it is no use

Not just another calendar

An ordinary shared calendar lets you write down events. Google already does that. The problem for a couple is not the events: it is that the logistics live in five different places — the work calendar, the shopping note, the chat thread with the links for the trip, and whatever one of you remembers to tell the other.

InSync puts all of it on the same screen.

Work shifts

Rotating ones included: set the pattern once and the month fills itself in, with exceptions when you swap a shift.

Shopping and errands

The list sorted by shop and the chores at home, shared. What one of you ticks off, the other sees.

Trips with an itinerary

Day by day, with journeys, bookings and whatever files you want to hand.

Notes with checklists

For the things that are not events but must not be forgotten either.

Widget and Siri

Your day at a glance on the home screen, and events created by voice.

Works with no signal

Everything is kept on the phone. When the network comes back, it catches up on its own.

What it does not do

No ads, your data is not sold, and it never asks for your location. If you attach a photo to a trip, the coordinates are stripped out before it is uploaded. What it collects and what it does not is written out in full in the privacy policy, with no small print.
